How these are calculated
Clicks
Every human visit through one of your links in the selected days. Counted once per visit. Automated traffic is filtered out before it is stored.
Unique clicks
Clicks from a different person, not the same visitor again. A click is unique when the same link has not seen the same visitor in the previous 30 minutes.
Orders
Paid orders that were attributed to one of your links. Only orders that were paid inside the range. A refunded order leaves this count.
Conversion rate
How many unique clicks turned into a paid order. Orders divided by unique clicks, shown as a percentage.
Earnings per 100 clicks
The commission you earned for every 100 clicks on your links. Commission divided by clicks, then multiplied by 100. It is a guide, not a promise.
Commission earned
Your share of the paid orders in the range. The product's commission percentage of each paid order, added up.
Pending balance
Commission that is still inside the hold period. Commission credited but not yet released. The hold is set by the platform, and the Earnings page states the current number of days.
Available balance
Commission that has cleared the hold. Everything credited minus everything paid out and minus what is still pending, never below zero.
